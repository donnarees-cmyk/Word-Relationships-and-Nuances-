Passage: "The Antique Collector"

Old Mr. Vance was known throughout the town as a frugal buyer. While some shop owners called him stingy behind his back, his friends insisted he was merely thrifty with his savings.

Inside his workshop, he inspected every artifact with a scrutinizing eye. Where an amateur might give a quick glance, Vance remained inquisitive, asking endless questions about an item's origin without seeming nosy.

When negotiating prices, he was remarkably steadfast in his offers. Sellers who didn't understand him considered him stubborn, but Vance simply respected the true value of craftsmanship.

Word Nuance Reference Matrix

Negative Connotation

Definition: Words that carry an unfavorable or critical emotional association.

Examples: Stingy (unwilling to share), Nosy (prying inappropriately), Stubborn (unreasonably unyielding).

Neutral Denotation

Definition: The literal, dictionary definition of a word without emotional bias.

Examples: Inexpensive (low cost), Curious (eager to learn), Persistent (continuing firmly).

Positive Connotation

Definition: Words that evoke favorable, admiring, or respectful emotional associations.

Examples: Frugal / Thrifty (wise with money), Inquisitive (intellectually curious), Steadfast (loyal & firm).
